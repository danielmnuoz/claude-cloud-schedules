Iran War / Middle East

  • Status: Post-ceasefire, fragile negotiations: The US-Israel-Iran war (began February 28) ended with an April 7–8 ceasefire. A formal Memorandum of Understanding was signed June 17 by the US and Iranian presidents, giving 60 days to finalize a permanent settlement — the clock runs out mid-August.
  • Doha talks show "positive progress": As of July 2, indirect US-Iran talks in Qatar made headway. But Iran's FM Araghchi warned of an "immediate powerful response" to any Israeli action — this remains the primary tail risk to markets.
  • Hormuz is the pivotal sticking point: The MoU commits Iran to "best efforts" for toll-free Hormuz passage for only 60 days. UAE has restored 3.9 mb/d exports; total Hormuz flows exceed 10 mb/d. A negotiations collapse would spike oil and risk-off across markets sharply.

Trade & Tariffs

  • Mexico tariff escalation: The Trump administration announced reciprocal tariffs on Mexico will rise to 30% by August 1. Separately, the formal USMCA review process was initiated in July — the US Trade Representative signaled a "rubberstamp is not in the national interest."
  • Critical July 24 deadline — Section 122 expiry: The 10% global baseline tariff authority under Section 122 expires July 24 unless Congress acts. Congress is split between extending, raising rates permanently, or letting it lapse — a major uncertainty for supply chains and import-exposed equities.
  • Section 301 forced labor hearings open July 7: Public hearings on proposed new duties tied to forced labor enforcement practices begin next week, with significant Asia sourcing implications.
