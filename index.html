Iran War & Middle East (Developing)

  • Ceasefire fragile — talks resumed: The US-Iran ceasefire (brokered by Pakistan on April 7–8, ending the February 28 US-Israel air war against Iran) remains under strain. Iran suspended talks on June 1 citing Israel's continued Lebanon offensive as a "ceasefire violation"; a regional source told CNN negotiations resumed within hours. Iran's attacks on Kuwait's airport and Bahrain on June 2–3 were condemned across the Middle East.
  • Strait of Hormuz brinkmanship: Iran threatened to "completely" block the Strait after talks broke down, briefly spiking oil and the dollar. Trump said a deal to reopen the Strait is "largely negotiated" and could be announced within the next week. Tehran has made a ceasefire in Lebanon a condition for any lasting agreement with Washington.
  • Domestic US pressure: The House passed a resolution limiting Trump's war powers in Iran; the Pentagon, State Department, and USAID inspectors general launched a joint review of the operation (which has exceeded 60 days as required by law).
  • Trajectory: A formal deal is possible in days but depends on halting Israeli operations in Lebanon. Escalation risk remains elevated; any credible Hormuz blockade would send oil sharply higher and rattle global markets.

Tech & Business

AI-Driven Layoff Wave

  • 134,600+ cuts in 2026 to date across 212 layoff events (~874/day average) — a 66% increase over the same period in 2025. AI was cited as the primary driver for the third consecutive month in May.
  • Major culprits: Oracle led with 30,000 cuts; Meta 8,000; Intuit ~3,000; Groupon 400. Meta, Amazon, and Oracle are collectively redirecting savings into a $700B AI infrastructure buildout — cutting headcount to fund compute.
  • Skills impact: Entry-level software roles (boilerplate coding, scripted testing, routine bug fixes) have fallen ~20% for developers ages 22–25 since 2024. ML infrastructure, model evaluation, and AI safety roles remain largely unfilled.

AI & Chip Industry

  • Broadcom's AI guidance miss rattles the sector: While AI chip revenue doubled YoY, the below-consensus Q3 outlook raised questions about whether peak AI capex demand is already priced in and whether hyperscaler custom chip budgets may be plateauing. The broader semiconductor index fell sharply in sympathy.
  • SK Hynix crosses $1 trillion: The South Korean memory giant surpassed a $1T market cap on HBM chip dominance, becoming the clearest beneficiary of global data-center AI buildout even as the broader chip index sold off on Broadcom's news.
