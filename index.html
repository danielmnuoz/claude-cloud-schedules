Iran / Middle East Conflict

  • Latest (Jul 7): An LNG tanker was struck and set ablaze near Limah, Oman — the first shipping attack in the Strait of Hormuz since the June 29 stand-down. No casualties reported, but it signals the ceasefire remains fragile and interdiction risk has not gone away.
  • Context: The U.S.–Israel "Operation Epic Fury" (launched Feb 28, 2026) formally ended May 5 after killing Supreme Leader Khamenei. A ceasefire was agreed April 7–8; a 60-day peace MOU was signed June 17. The conflict has now shifted to a Strait of Hormuz brinkmanship phase, with the U.S. reinstating sanctions on Iranian oil following a series of tanker attacks on commercial shipping.
  • Implication: Renewed interdiction risk keeps a geopolitical premium in crude. Extended disruptions could materially worsen already-elevated inflation readings and complicate the Fed's path.

Upstream wafer and materials suppliers remain less crowded than headline transceiver names.
