Iran War & Middle East — Latest (May 6)

  • Ceasefire holding: The US-Iran ceasefire brokered by Pakistan on April 8 remains in place, now approaching 30 days. Trump announced Wednesday that "Project Freedom" — the US Navy's Strait of Hormuz reopening operation — is paused, citing "great progress" toward a "complete and final agreement" with Iran.
  • Iran's 14-point counter-proposal (submitted May 2) demands include: war end within 30 days (vs. the US-proposed 60-day ceasefire), US forces withdrawal from Iran's periphery, end of naval blockade, frozen asset release, reparations, sanctions removal, and a new Hormuz governance mechanism.
  • Trump's position: Said he is "not satisfied" with Iran's terms but is pausing military operations to allow talks to continue. An Axios report that the two sides are near a one-page framework memo drove the morning's market rally.
  • Key sticking points: Iran's nuclear program and long-term Strait of Hormuz governance remain unresolved. A breakthrough would be a significant positive shock to global markets; a breakdown would push oil back above $100+.
  • Lebanon: Israel and Hezbollah continue trading fire despite a separately declared ceasefire; Lebanese authorities report over 2,600 killed and 1M+ displaced in 2026.

Trade Policy & Diplomacy

  • US-China tariffs average ~33% as a trade-weighted rate; stacked layers push some sectors (EVs, batteries, solar) to effective rates near 145%. The 90-day tariff truce extension runs through August 2026.
  • China publicly denied deliberately pursuing a trade surplus and reaffirmed commitment to rules-based global trade. New US Section 301 investigations into China, Vietnam, Taiwan, Mexico, Japan, and the EU were launched in March 2026.

Tech & Business

Layoffs & Restructuring

  • Big Tech shed 80,000+ jobs in early 2026, with over 119,000 total tech sector layoffs recorded YTD (~960/day). Companies broadly cite AI-driven efficiency, though OpenAI's Sam Altman noted many firms are "AI-washing" standard cost cuts.
  • Meta announced ~10% workforce reduction (~8,000 jobs) as it redirects capital toward AI infrastructure; Amazon cut ~16,000 corporate roles in Q1 (more than half of all Q1 tech layoffs); Microsoft offered voluntary retirement to ~7% of its US workforce.
  • Cognizant is evaluating 12,000–15,000 global job cuts as AI accelerates automation of IT services work.

AI & Product Developments

  • AMD's Helios: The chipmaker confirmed plans to ship its first full rack-scale AI system later in 2026, competing with Nvidia's Grace Blackwell and Vera Rubin platforms (priced $3M+). OpenAI and Meta are already signed up for shipments.
  • AMD server CPU market: Expects 35%+ market growth YoY; CPU share ratio approaching 1:1 with Intel. Company projects a $120B total addressable CPU market by 2030 driven by AI workloads.
  • DeepSeek continued to pressure model inference pricing with long-context, low-cost releases — forcing broader industry competition on cost-per-token and expanding the range of viable AI use cases.

Funding & Acquisitions

  • Reserv (insurtech) closed a $125M Series C led by KKR; uses generative AI to automate insurance claims processing for insurers and TPAs.
  • VCs poured $18.8B into AI startups founded since early 2025. Investors are favoring AI tied to real-world verticals — robotics, manufacturing, defense, enterprise SaaS — over thin consumer plays.
