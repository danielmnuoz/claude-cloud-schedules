Iran War — Developing Story (Day ~122)

  • Background: US–Israel–Iran hostilities began February 28, 2026. A ceasefire was agreed April 7–8, and a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) was signed by Presidents Trump and Iran's president on June 17, setting a 60-day window for a full peace agreement.
  • Latest escalation: Iran attacked a commercial vessel near the Strait of Hormuz (~June 23); the US struck Iranian missile/drone storage and radar sites on June 27. Iran's IRGC retaliated by targeting US military bases in Qatar, Kuwait, UAE, and the US Fifth Fleet HQ in Bahrain.
  • Diplomacy: Trump called the Iranian attack a "foolish violation" of the MOU but confirmed peace talks resume today in Doha. The Strait of Hormuz was briefly re-closed June 20 by Iran (citing Israeli strikes in Lebanon) but has partially reopened. Trajectory: fragile — Doha talks are the key near-term variable.

Trade Policy

  • The Supreme Court ruled on February 20 that IEEPA does not authorize presidential tariffs, invalidating "Liberation Day" reciprocal tariffs; the administration pivoted to a Section 122 flat 10% import surcharge, which the Court of International Trade also struck down — but it remains collected under appeal, with authority expiring July 24.
  • USTR proposed a 25% tariff on select Brazilian goods (June 1) and launched Section 301 investigations on 60 countries for forced-labor-linked imports; a US–China bilateral trade board process is ongoing with a comment period open.
