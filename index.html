International Markets & Geopolitics

Iran War — Latest Developments

  • US struck "multiple targets in Iran" on June 9–10. The strikes ended swiftly. Trump warned the US could resume attacks, blaming slow progress in negotiations.
  • A conditional ceasefire remains in place but is fragile. UN Secretary General Guterres called it "more like a lesser-fire" and urged a diplomatic settlement.
  • Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed: 95% reduction in crude oil shipping, 99% reduction in LNG since the conflict began in February. This is the single largest supply disruption to global energy markets in decades.

ECB Rate Decision — Today

  • The ECB is expected to hike rates by 25 bps to 2.25% — its first rate hike in nearly three years. Market pricing implies 100% probability.
  • Eurozone inflation jumped to 3.2% in May (energy prices +10.9%, services +3.5%), forcing the ECB's hand.
  • Watch Lagarde's press conference (12:45 GMT) for guidance on whether this is a one-off precautionary move or the start of a tightening cycle.
