Market Pulse — Daily Briefing

Friday, August 28, 2026

US Markets & Finance

Major Index Movements

  • S&P 500 & Nasdaq rallied on August 28 as tech led gains following blowout earnings from Nvidia and Salesforce. The Nasdaq Composite climbed ~1.57% to 26,541 and the Dow added ~0.20% to 53,569, capping a strong week.
  • Treasury yields edged higher — the 10-year reached 4.67% — but investor confidence held firm, with the VIX declining as risk appetite improved.

Notable Stock Movers & Earnings

  • Nvidia (NVDA) reported fiscal Q2 FY2027 earnings of $2.22/share (more than double YoY) on revenue of $96.2B (+106% YoY), guiding Q3 to $106–$110B. CEO Jensen Huang projected 70%+ fiscal 2028 revenue growth constrained by supply, not demand. Shares surged after-hours Wednesday and continued higher Thursday.
  • Salesforce (CRM) jumped 11–18% after crushing estimates: adj. EPS $5.90 vs. $3.27 expected; revenue $11.35B (+11% YoY). Agentforce + Data 360 ARR surpassed $3.9B (+210% YoY). Full-year adj. EPS guidance raised to $16.67–$16.71, up ~18.5% at the midpoint.
  • Photonics names (LITE, COHR, AAOI) extended gains on the back of Nvidia's results and broader AI capex momentum; AAOI is up 390% YTD, LITE +138%, COHR +107%.

Fed / Monetary Policy

  • Fed Chair Kevin Warsh delivered his first Jackson Hole keynote as chair on August 28 at 10am ET. Markets were on edge given Warsh's break from the traditional practice of telegraphing rate moves. He is under intense pressure to clarify his stance on reaccelerating inflation and the path for rate hikes.
  • Rates on hold at 3.50–3.75% following the July 28–29 FOMC meeting. The next decision is September 16. Warsh has announced five Fed task forces to examine areas central to monetary policy conduct.
  • Inflation concern: the key question is whether Warsh views 2026's reaccelerated inflation as temporary or whether he signals a willingness to hike. His comments Friday are likely to be the defining market catalyst of the week.

Economic Data

  • Gold's surge to $4,602/oz (+34.7% YoY) reflects ongoing dollar-debasement concerns linked to Treasury buyback expansions, which may signal broader macro unease heading into fall.

International Markets & Geopolitics

Global Market Movements

  • Asia was mixed but broadly positive; Korea and mainland China outperformed, while Hong Kong and India lagged. Markets tracked Nvidia and Salesforce post-earnings gains overnight into the Asia open.
  • Europe held steady with modest gains; markets largely await Warsh's Jackson Hole speech as the next global rates catalyst.
  • Brazil's Ibovespa hit 175,135 (+0.31%), posting its fifth straight daily gain as LatAm markets benefited from commodity strength.

Iran War & Middle East

  • Iran conflict update: The US-Israel war against Iran (begun February 28, 2026) appears to be decelerating. A Pakistan-mediated ceasefire and June MOU halted large-scale fighting, but both sides continue to trade strikes over alleged violations of the accord.
  • Core disputes remain unresolved: Iran's nuclear program, Strait of Hormuz access, and sanctions relief are all still contested. A durable settlement is not in sight.
  • Gulf oil export routes have recovered to ~two-thirds of pre-war levels; shipping continues to route around the Strait of Hormuz and Red Sea.

Trade Policy & Sanctions

  • US-Canada tariffs: A 50% tariff on $20B of Canadian exports took effect August 22 after a brief delay; US-Canada trade talks remain suspended with Mexico facing pressure to match Canada's terms.
  • Iran sanctions: Treasury Secretary Bessent vowed the "toughest sanctions in history" on Iran; the US has also issued a framework for secondary tariffs on nations purchasing Iranian goods or services.
  • China: US eyes additional 7.5% overcapacity tariffs ahead of rumored Xi-Trump talks; Trump reportedly banned Chinese imports of data center components, boosting domestic optical/connectivity names.

Commodities & Currencies

  • Crude oil at ~$83.10/bbl (-0.51% day; +29.8% YoY) as Persian Gulf export recovery reaches ~two-thirds capacity; Goldman Sachs noted continued rerouting pressure.
  • Gold surged to $4,602/oz (+14.3% past month; +34.7% YoY) driven by dollar-debasement fears tied to Treasury's expanded long-bond buyback program.
  • USD edged slightly higher alongside yields; the dollar index remains elevated amid tariff-related safe-haven flows.

Tech & Business

  • AI-driven layoffs: 548 tech layoffs in 2026 have cut ~176,000 jobs (760/day). Amazon, Oracle, Meta, and Microsoft account for ~50,000 of the ~140,000 US cuts. Microsoft cut 4,800 roles (July 9), Meta laid off 8,000 (~10% of workforce) while moving 7,000 into AI roles, and Cisco is cutting ~4,000 despite record revenue of $15.8B.
  • Oracle: AI automation cost 21,000 jobs this year, with more restructuring signaled; the company is leaning into its cloud/AI infrastructure buildout.
  • Salesforce Agentforce: The company's AI agent platform crossed $3.9B ARR (+210% YoY), its fastest-growing product ever, and is reshaping how enterprise software companies pitch AI ROI to skeptical CFOs.
  • Stripe acquires OpenRouter in its largest-ever acquisition, betting on AI model mix and the token economy — a significant signal that payment infrastructure is moving to support AI agent transactions natively.
  • Anthropic IPO watch: Reports indicate Anthropic filed confidentially for a public offering; Polymarket gives ~46% odds of an Anthropic IPO by September 2026. The company's valuation is a stake held by Salesforce (reflected in CRM's $2.6B investment gain this quarter).
