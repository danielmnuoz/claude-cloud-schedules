Iran Conflict — Latest (Developing)

  • US-Iran ceasefire collapsed July 9. The April 7 ceasefire — reached after five weeks of fighting sparked by US-Israeli strikes that killed Supreme Leader Khamenei on February 28, 2026 — broke down as both sides resumed strikes. The US has conducted more than 170 airstrikes on Iran in two days. Iran fired 10 ballistic missiles at a US military base in northern Jordan (intercepted by Jordanian defenses).
  • Strait of Hormuz severely disrupted: Iran struck two commercial tankers (al-Bahiya and Mombasa) in Omani territorial waters near the Strait. Daily transits have collapsed from ~110 ships/day pre-war to roughly 13 in the past 24 hours. An Indian crew member aboard the Mombasa was killed. Roughly 20% of global oil supply transits the Strait; sustained disruption is the chief oil-price and inflation risk.
  • Trajectory: No diplomatic off-ramp visible. The core market concern is an oil-price spiral feeding into inflation, which locks the Fed into a tighter stance at a time when growth is already slowing.

  • Effective US-China tariff rate remains ~30% — the highest of any major trading partner — keeping supply-chain uncertainty elevated for multinationals and semiconductor equipment makers.
