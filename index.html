US–Iran War — Day 65 (Developing)

  • Background: The war launched February 28, 2026, when US and Israeli forces struck Iranian military and government targets; Iran subsequently closed the Strait of Hormuz. A ceasefire was agreed April 7–8 but remains fragile.
  • 14-point Iranian counter-proposal: Iran submitted a counter-proposal on May 2 demanding resolution of all issues and a formal end to the war within 30 days — a tighter timeline than the two-month ceasefire extension the US had proposed. Trump publicly called the offer unsatisfactory and said the US is reviewing it.
  • Project Freedom + Hormuz incident (today): Trump launched "Project Freedom" — a US naval escort mission for commercial vessels stranded in the Strait of Hormuz. Iran's IRGC warned any US forces entering the strait would be attacked. Iranian state media (Fars News, IRGC) reported two missiles struck a US warship near Jask; the US military denied any ship was hit. Markets briefly sold off on the reports before partially recovering.
  • Lebanon front: Israel and Hezbollah continue to trade fire despite a separately declared ceasefire, with 2,000+ killed in the 2026 Lebanon conflict.
  • Trajectory: Ceasefire collapse risk is elevated. A confirmed US–Iran naval engagement would likely push Brent above $120/bbl. A credible Hormuz reopening deal, conversely, could trigger an immediate 20–30% oil price drop and meaningfully ease global inflation.

Tech & Business

  • Meta layoffs (effective May 20): Meta plans to cut ~8,000 jobs (~10% of global workforce) starting May 20, plus cancel 6,000 open roles. The company cited a restructuring into AI-focused "pods" while committing to $115–135B in AI infrastructure capex in 2026 alone.

ARI's technology focuses on robots that understand, predict, and adapt to human behavior — bolstering Meta's humanoid AI ambitions alongside its existing Reality Labs hardware division.
  • Apple Intelligence validated: Apple's Q2 beat is the first large-scale data point confirming on-device AI features drive hardware upgrade cycles at consumer scale — iPhone revenue surged ~22% YoY. This is a meaningful signal for the broader thesis that AI is a hardware upgrade catalyst, not only an enterprise software story.
